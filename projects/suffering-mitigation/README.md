# Suffering mitigation

License: MIT; see the [root license](../../LICENSE).

This project is a proposed **allocation** demonstration in Praesidium
Humanitatis's [post-profit economics program](../../docs/post-profit-economics.md).
Its research question is how limited response resources can be assigned to
human needs through accountable decisions and evaluated for their effect on
suffering.

The proposed model uses evidence of need, explicit resource constraints and
reviewable priorities to support allocation. A concrete setting, governance
process and allocation rule remain to be defined with affected people. The
[Temporal Fusion Transformer](../../tools/temporal-fusion-transformer/README.md)
is a candidate forecasting tool for that work. Forecast accuracy, the quality
of allocation decisions and evidence of beneficial intervention are separate
tests.

Formalization must identify the resources and competing uses, who may decide,
how unmet need and uncertainty are represented, and how decisions can be
reviewed. Compare the proposed allocation mechanism with existing practice and
simple needs-based rules under comparable resource limits. Evaluate who receives
help, who remains underserved and whether the intervention reduces suffering.

The reusable TFT remains independent of this application. Define the population,
consent and data provenance, measurable target, feasible response, and evaluation
of benefit with affected people before deploying a decision system. Do not use a
forecast as a substitute for a person's right to assistance or human review.

The existing [research plan](../../docs/research-plan.md) and
[TFT replication gate](../../docs/tft-replication.md) remain authoritative for
the forecasting work. The project is at the research-definition stage; no
implemented allocation mechanism, application deployment or humanitarian
effectiveness is claimed.
